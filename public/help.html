How to use MicroLend

Swipe through the flow, then open the matching screen. KYC unlocks scoring, scoring unlocks a loan, Razorpay disburses it.

Step 1

Verify KYC

Submit Aadhaar or PAN. Format is checked — 12-digit checksum or PAN like ABCPE1234F.

Step 2

Generate a credit score

Enter income, debt, and employment. You get a score and an approved INR limit.

Step 3

Request a loan

On the dashboard, request an amount within your limit. A Razorpay order is created.

Step 4

Pay to disburse

Open the loan and complete test checkout. Capture updates status to disbursed.
