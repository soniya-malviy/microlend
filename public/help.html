1

Verify KYC

Open KYC and submit Aadhaar or PAN with the name on the ID. This demo cannot call UIDAI or NSDL, so we only check format: 12-digit Aadhaar (checksum) or a 10-character PAN whose 4th letter is P.

Try Aadhaar 2345 6789 0124 or PAN ABCPE1234F.

Go to KYC
2

Generate a credit score

After KYC is verified, open Credit score. Enter monthly income, existing debt, and employment type. The engine returns a score and an approved INR limit.

Scoring stays locked until KYC is verified.

Go to credit score
3

Request a loan

On the Dashboard, tap Request disbursement. Enter an amount up to your approved limit. That creates a Razorpay order; the loan stays pending until payment is captured.

The request button stays disabled until you have a limit.

Go to dashboard
4

Pay to disburse

Open the loan from Loans and tap Pay securely. Complete Razorpay test checkout with UPI, card, or netbanking. When the webhook confirms capture, status moves to disbursed.

Razorpay test card: 4111 1111 1111 1111, any future expiry, any CVV.

Go to loans
